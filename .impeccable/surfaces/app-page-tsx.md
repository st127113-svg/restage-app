## Direction contract

THESIS: The redesign journey as a walk down a hardware superstore aisle, not another glossy AI-photo hero. Every phase is a numbered department; every decision is priced on the spot. Refuses the before/after-slider hero (the category default, and this app's current look) and the sterile SaaS-dashboard opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Restrained palette — warehouse white `#F2F2EF` and charcoal ink `#1E211D` as the ground, one committed accent, safety yellow `#F5B700`, spent only on price and primary action (palette-as-law, donated by the declined arcade challenger); budget state carried by green `#2F7A4D` / red `#C1432D`, nowhere else. Display/signage face **Archivo Black** for aisle numbers, department headers, and price numerals; body/UI face **IBM Plex Sans**. Die-cut price-tag components, SKU-style shelf tags for items, barcode-strip dividers, aisle-directory header. Light, daylight-retail scene, never dark.

STORY: The visitor walks a real, priced route from photo to plan — like shopping their own room at a hardware superstore — but the generated redesign and style discovery stay the emotional peak: the aisle system frames and prices the inspiration, it never buries it under shelf-tag chrome.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An aisle-directory header names the five phases as departments (current one lit). Below it, related stages consolidate into single aisle bays — e.g. room type + purpose + space share one screen instead of three — closing the "aggregate page" gap. The upload zone reads as a drop-off counter; its CTA is a die-cut yellow price tag. The Design phase (style pick, generated image, before/after result) is the one department that breaks the shelf-tag grid: full-bleed photography, the aisle system receding to a quiet frame so the redesign itself reads as the reward, price tags returning once the visitor moves into Plan.

FORM: Aisle Signage (HomePro/ThaiWatsadu hardware superstore signage), grounded candidate 5 of 7, assigned by the roll. Raised by four declined challengers: deep-gutter section breaks (cloud quarry), the budget slider as a one-pull gather gesture (silk cape), palette-as-law (CRT arcade), tactile pressed-in price tags and buttons (CD-ROM console). Seed key 23ec7ec9.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
